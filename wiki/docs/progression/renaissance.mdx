# Renaissance

The **Renaissance Altar** (crafted with crying obsidian, amethyst shards and a respawn anchor) is your permanent progression. It has five tabs: Sacrifice, Shop, Forge, Heritage and Vocations.

## Renaissance Points (RP)

- Earned by **playing**: 1 RP per wave cleared (2 per Assault survived in Kingdom), +1 per boss, +5 on victory, and +15 the first time you win a given horde.
- Multiplied by difficulty (×0.75 / ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2) and mutators, then by personal bonuses (+5% per rank, Merchant node 4, Fortune set).
- A defeat keeps the wave and boss RP but gives no victory bonus; instead it pays a **consolation prize**: 30% of the victory bonuses, scaled by the waves survived (adjustable). Stopping a horde with /ws stop gives nothing.
- You can also **sacrifice** items of good rarity for RP.

## Shop and Forge

Spend RP on permanent items and stats in the Shop. The Forge upgrades and fuses relics (see the Relics page).

## Heritage tree

Three branches of 9 nodes, unlocked in order for **5 / 10 / 15 / 25 / 40 / 50 / 60 / 75 / 90 RP**. Branches no longer grant stats (those are in the 4th column, **Body**, also available in the Shop): they change how you play. The Lord branch also strengthens blessings (+5%, +10%, +15%).
