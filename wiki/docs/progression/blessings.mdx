# Blessings & Pacts

## Blessings

- After each wave you pick **one of 3 blessings** (4 with the Lord's Heritage, node 3). In Kingdom mode, every Calm offers them.
- Picking the same blessing again raises its **level** and multiplies its effect.
- You keep them if you die, and they are **dispelled when the horde ends**.
- `/ws benediction liste` shows the full catalogue.

## Blood Pacts

- A Pact is a special **head item**. Hold it and **right-click**: it rolls a random weighted effect, then goes on cooldown.
- A **sealed** pact grants all its effects at once, and sealing it summons the horde.
- `/ws pacte list` shows the pacts, `/ws pacte resetcd` resets your cooldown.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
