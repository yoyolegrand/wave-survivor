# Relics & Forge

Relics are unique items with permanent effects. You find them in **roulette chests**, with **chaos merchants**, and with the **Sealed Reliquary**.

## Equipping

- **Only equipped relics work.** Put them in the Curios **Relic** slot or, without Curios, equip them at the **Renaissance Altar › Forge › Equipment**: 3 slots, +1 per Renaissance rank (6 max).

## Levels

- A relic has **level I, II (×1.5) or III (×2)** of its effects.

## Forge (Renaissance Altar)

- **Upgrade**: a duplicate of the relic (consumed) plus RP: **15 RP** for level II, **30 RP** for level III.
- **Fuse**: two specific relics of the same family, both **level II or higher**, plus **50 RP** give one of the 12 **legendary relics**. A legendary acts as its two parents at level II (so it counts as 2 set pieces) and adds a unique effect.
- **Sealed Reliquary**: sold in the Renaissance shop (40 RP by default). Right-click for a random base relic, handy to find duplicates for the Forge.
