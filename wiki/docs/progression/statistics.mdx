# Statistics

Your statistics and achievements, saved with the world: `/ws stats` or the altar's **"Statistics"** button.

## Statistics

- Hordes started and completed, waves cleared, monsters and bosses killed, deaths, time spent in hordes, Boss Rush, Daily Challenges, best Endless wave.

## Achievements

- These are real Minecraft advancements: open them with the **L** key, in the Wave Survivor tab.
- They unlock on their own when you reach the thresholds. Counters start with 1.7: nothing is retroactive.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
