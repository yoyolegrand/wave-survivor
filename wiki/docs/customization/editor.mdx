# Horde editor

Operators only: `/ws editor`.

**• New horde**: start from a template (Short classic, Long classic, Kingdom or Empty).
**• ✔ Check**: lists problems; click one to jump to the right tab.
**• Preview**: content of each wave, « ▶ Test wave » to spawn it, « ✖ Clear » to remove it.
**• 📤 Export / 📥 Import**: share a complete horde as a .zip (config/wavesurvivor/export and import).
**• 📋 Copy / Paste**: hordes, mobs and bosses through the clipboard.
**• Entities & skills**: custom monsters and skills.
**• Boss skills**: new types (ice spikes, frost nova, blizzard, glacier pillars, shatter), all configurable.
**• "Storm" chaos**: configurable storm zone (duration, damage, wind, particles, shelter blocks, effects).
**• Special waves**: "Excluded waves" to prevent a special wave on given waves ("1" by default).
**• Boss Rush**: dedicated settings view (Calm between bosses, etc.).
**• Desert skills**: sandstorm, quicksand, dust devil, star fracture, earthquake.
**• "Earthquake" chaos** and storm options: shelter under cover or near heat, snow or sand look, immunity relic.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
