# Frozen Peaks

A frozen horde: blizzards, frost creatures and three fearsome bosses. It unlocks after the Necropolis, and its altar is bound with **one blue ice and four snow blocks**.

## Atmosphere

- Everything here **slows and chills**: that is intended. Keep a way to warm up.
- A **storm** can sweep the area: a lit campfire shelters you.
- The cold builds up: the frost bar fills, and frostbite eventually hurts. Leather boots protect against basic freezing.
- Fire hurts frost golems.

## Threats

- Some desecrators ignore players: they rush the **Monolith**. Protect it.
- Wolves hunt in packs, led by an Alpha.
- The yeti hurls ice boulders and shakes the ground.

## The three bosses

- **Krag the Ice-Breaker**: a colossus that shatters the ice beneath your feet.
- **Syllis**: a supreme snow sorceress. Her blizzard follows her everywhere.
- **Aurvang**: the Ancient. His ice splits players apart… and only breaks with teamwork.

Each has its secrets: observe, keep moving, and don't stay alone.
