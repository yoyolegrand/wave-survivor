# Burning Sands

A sun-scorched desert: mummies, scarabs, scorpions and jackals prowl the ruins. It unlocks after the Necropolis, and its altar is bound with **one chiseled sandstone and four red sand**.

## Atmosphere

- **Sandstorms** rise: exposed players are hurt, slowed and parched. Get **under cover** (under a roof, or in water) to escape them.
- The ground can **shake**: cracks run across the floor and rubble falls. No shelter helps: watch where you step.
- The **Desert Veil** relic (boss loot) protects from sandstorms and quicksand, and makes you faster.

## Threats

- Some desecrators rush the **Monolith**. Protect it.
- Jackals hunt in packs, scarabs come by the dozens, scorpions poison.
- Riders, mummified royal guards, sandstone colossi and djinns (with their spirits and fangs) reinforce the waves.

## The three bosses

- **Anubis, Tomb Guardian**: master of mummies and curses.
- **Sekhmet, Queen of Scorpions**: she burrows, poisons and traps.
- **Ra-Atum, the Ancient of Sands**: he commands the storm, the whirlwind and the earthquake.

Each has its secrets: observe, keep moving, and keep your Veil.
