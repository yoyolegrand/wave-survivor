# Classic hordes

Monsters arrive in **waves**, each bigger than the last. More players means more and tougher monsters.

## As the game goes on

- **Special waves**: unusual line-ups, announced in chat.
- **Bosses**: on specific waves (see the Bosses page).
- **Merchants**: villagers offering trades.
- **Pauses**: breathers between some waves.
- **Chaos events**: ore deposits to mine, gravity anomalies, totems, breaches… mid-fight.
- **Roulette chests**: mystery chests opened with keys dropped by monsters.

## Forced wave end

When only a few monsters remain, they glow through walls and a countdown starts. At zero, stragglers vanish without loot and the game goes on.

## Victory

Survive the last wave to claim the rewards.

## Roulette chests & keys

Monsters drop **keys**. Right-click a Roulette Chest with a matching key to spin it (left-click previews its loot). Reliquary keys open the themed chests: Boss, Treasures, Nether, End, Abyss and Arcane.

## Leaderboard

At the end of a horde, the top 3 players and the boss killer receive rewards.
