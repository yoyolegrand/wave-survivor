# Bosses

Bosses appear on specific waves and are announced in chat. Some summon minions, surround themselves with **protective totems** (destroy them to make the boss vulnerable) or wait in their **arena**: a portal then opens near the altar after the last wave.
A boss can also carry affixes that change how it fights.

Powerful attacks are **announced**: zones that light up, lines on the ground, particles. Watch the floor and move. Some mechanics require you to **work together**.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
