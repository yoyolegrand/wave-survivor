# Difficulty & Mutators

Difficulty and mutators are chosen at the altar before launching. The harder it gets, the better the rewards. Some mutators are ignored in Kingdom mode.

## Difficulties

- **Easy**: Weaker, fewer monsters and bosses. Reduced rewards.
- **Normal**: The experience intended by the horde's creator.
- **Hard**: Tougher, more numerous monsters and stronger bosses. Increased rewards.
- **Nightmare**: For the boldest: everything is much stronger… and much more generous.

## Mutators

- **Frenzy**: Monsters are 30% faster.
- **Armored**: Monsters have 50% more HP.
- **Swarm**: 50% more monsters per wave.
- **Volatile**: Monsters explode when they die.
- **Venomous**: Every hit taken from a monster poisons.
- **Fragile**: No natural health regeneration.
- **Hungry**: Hunger drops 2× faster.
- **One Life**: Dying = spectator until the horde ends.
- **Enraged**: Bosses: +50% HP, end-of-fight mechanics from the start.
- **Escort**: Each boss arrives with elite minions.
- **Eternal Night**: Permanent darkness over the arena.
- **Unstable Ground**: An Anomaly every wave.
- **Glass Monolith**: The Monolith's integrity is halved.
- **Brutes**: Monsters ×1.5 HP and damage, but slower (and bigger with Pehkui).
- **Runts**: Monsters at half HP, but very fast (and smaller with Pehkui).

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
