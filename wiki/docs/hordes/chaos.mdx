# Breaches, Anomalies & Chaos

Hordes throw extra threats at you in the middle of the fight.

## Breaches

- Rifts open around the Monolith and keep spawning **reinforcements** until destroyed.
- Destroying one gives a reward. A Breach still open at the end of a wave makes the horde **stronger**.
- Each horde has its own Breach types (monsters, rewards, looks).

## Anomalies

- Glowing circles appear under players and elsewhere in the arena. They turn **red** just before activating, then apply their effects (levitation, slowness, poison, pull…) to **players**.
- **Step out of the circle** before it fires. Slow Falling and the Void Anchor Ring help.

## Chaos events

- **Totems** (Frenzy, Regeneration, Curse, Summoning) buff the horde until they are destroyed.
- **Ore deposits** and **fantasy trees**: mine or chop them for resources (a minimum tool tier may be needed).
- **Chaos merchants** sell rare and legendary items, sometimes only for a short time.
- **Storms** (some hordes): exposed players freeze and take damage. Stay close to a campfire or another heat source to be sheltered (in the desert: get under cover, under a roof or in water).
- **Earthquakes**: the ground shakes, cracks open and rubble falls. Nothing shelters you: dodge them.
