# Seraph Shrine

## The statue

- Ruins of quartz hold a **Seraph Shrine** (a statue with three pairs of wings, **unbreakable**). Right-click opens its screen.
- A single **faith** gauge (from **-600** to **+600**), shared by every player: **✦ Pure**, ✧ Soothed, ◇ Neutral, **✕ Tainted**, **☠ Corrupted**. The statue changes appearance with its state.

## Offerings (tab 1)

- Drop an item in the slot, then **Offer**. **Grace**: flowers, glow berries, honey, amethyst, white candle, gold, ender pearl, golden apples, totem. **Taint**: rotten flesh, bones, spider eye, gunpowder, soul sand, sculk, skulls, wither rose. Grace and taint offset each other.
- Near the statue (16 blocks): horde monster killed **+1**, peaceful animal killed by a player **-3**, **villager killed by a player -50**.

## Harvest and fusion (tabs 2 and 3)

- Pure or Soothed: the statue makes **Grace Shards**; Tainted or Corrupted: **Taint Shards** (Neutral: nothing). Horde monsters killed nearby speed it up. **Harvest** empties the stock (64 max).
- **Fusion**: 9 Shards → 1 Fragment, 9 Fragments → 1 Heart (grace or taint). Hearts can also drop from bosses.

## Forge (tab 4)

- Place a weapon or armor and fragments: **Insert** draws a random upgrade. **3 slots** per item, costing 1, 2 then 3 fragments; the fragment level (Shard, Fragment, Heart) sets the strength.
- **Consecrated** (grace fragments): purely positive. **Profaned** (taint fragments): powerful bonus but a **drawback**. **One alignment per item.** Removing an upgrade costs fragments (not refunded) and lets you reroll it.
- A bonus never exceeds **+30%** combined over all worn gear (except one stronger single item). Upgraded items get a **colored name**, an **animated frame** and details in the tooltip.

## Crafting (tab 5)

- Craft utility blocks and gear from your fragments: see the **Sacred & Profane Crafting** page.

## The price of corruption

- Tainted: **Rootlings** appear around the statue; Corrupted: more often, sometimes with a **Stone Brute** — as long as a player is within 32 blocks.

## Settings

- **config/wavesurvivor/shrine\_offerings.json** (offerings, thresholds, events, production, Corrupted) and **shrine\_upgrades.json** (upgrades, costs, cap): only put the fields to change, then `/ws reload`. Display option: **client\_options.json** › slotFx.

## The Stone Brute

- A colossus of mossy rocks, slow but immune to knockback. **Stone Skin**: beyond 6 damage, the rest of a hit is reduced by 40%. **Crushing Blow**: a punch that launches you into the air.
- **Boulder Toss**: rips a boulder from the ground and hurls it at distant enemies (area damage, Slowness). It aims between 7 and 20 blocks, every 12 s: don't back away in a straight line.
