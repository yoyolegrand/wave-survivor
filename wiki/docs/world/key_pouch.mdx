# Key Pouch

The **Key Pouch** stores your roulette chest keys and uses them for you.

## Contents

- Right-click in the air: opens the pouch (27 slots by default). Only **roulette chest keys** fit.
- The contents stay in the item: store it, drop it or give it away.

## Opening a chest

- **With no key in hand**, right-click a roulette chest: a key of the right type is taken from your pouch (anywhere in your inventory).
- **Sneak** + right-click: every key of that type is used, with a single fast animation and a summary screen of all prizes.
- A key held in hand always comes first.

## Auto pickup

- Keys you pick up go straight into the pouch. Sneak + right-click in the air to turn it on or off.

## Crafting

- 4 leather, 2 string, 1 gold ingot and 1 gold nugget (crafting table). Settings in **rules.json**: size, auto pickup, bulk opening.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
