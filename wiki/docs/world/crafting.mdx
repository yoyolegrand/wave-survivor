# Sacred & Profane Crafting

The Shrine's fragments (**Shards**, **Fragments** and **Hearts** of grace or taint) also craft food, blocks and gear. **Consecrated** (grace) = moderate, positive effects; **Profaned** (taint) = strong effects, **with a price**. The fragment level sets the power: Shards for common items, Fragments for useful ones, Hearts for rare ones.

## Food (crafting table)

- **Consecrated**: **Blessed Bread** (3 wheat + Shard), **Sanctified Apple** (apple + 4 Shards: Absorption, removes a negative effect), **Celestial Mead** (honey bottle + Fragment: Resistance), **Feast of Grace** (Heart + 3 blessed bread + 3 golden apples): a placeable block with **6 portions** the whole team can eat (8 hunger and 4 hearts each).
- **Profaned**: **Tainted Meat** (Strength, then Hunger), **Void Fruit** (Speed II, a little Nausea), **Black Wine** (+20% damage but -2 max hearts), **Raw Heart** (+40% damage but no natural regeneration).
- **Only one special food effect at a time**: eating a second one replaces the first.

## Decoration blocks (crafting table)

- **Blessed Marble** (8 calcite + Grace Shard) and **Tainted Stone** (8 deepslate + Taint Shard): stairs, slabs, walls, columns, polished and chiseled versions. They glow faintly. Also available at the stonecutter.

## Shrine Crafting tab

- The Shrine's fifth tab: pick a craft, check the cost (green = you have enough) and click **Craft**. Ingredients are taken from your inventory.
- **Grace utility blocks**: **Lantern of Grace** (nearby undead and Corrupted deal less damage), **Holy Fountain** (Regeneration around it, except during a boss fight), **Votive Candle** (lit: no natural monster spawns around it; horde waves still come).
- **Taint utility blocks**: **Bait Totem** (right click: lures monsters within 64 blocks for 30 s, 60 s cooldown, great for traps; 25% chance to summon a wave of zombies, skeletons, spiders and wither skeletons, outside of a game), **Profane Obelisk** (hurts monsters within 12 blocks, gives players Hunger), **Blood Altar** (right click: 4 hearts for experience).

## Gear

- **Consecrated**: **Dawnblade** (diamond sword, +50% against undead and Corrupted, glows at night), **Blessed Arrows** (set undead ablaze and knock them back), **Totem of Grace** (replaces death and heals nearby allies).
- **Profaned**: **Scythe of Taint** (wide sweep, life steal, **missed swing = -1 heart**), **Tainted Arrows** (Wither and Slowness), **Totem of Taint** (brings you back: +50% damage for 30 s, then only 4 max hearts for 2 min).
- Held in hand, totems replace death. The Dawnblade and the Scythe go through the Shrine's **Forge** but keep their alignment: a Dawnblade only takes grace, a Scythe only taint.
- **Seraph Armor** (consecrated): helm, chestplate, greaves and sabatons, crafted from a netherite piece and Grace Fragments (the chestplate also needs a Heart). A winged, golden 3D model. Wearing all 4 pieces gives the **Blessing of the Seraph**: **-15%** damage taken from undead and the Corrupted and **+2 hearts** of max health. Netherite-level protection and toughness, but more durable; with Iron's Spells each piece also gives +6% holy spell power and +25 max mana. Upgradable at the Shrine's Forge (grace fragments only).
- **Fallen Seraph Armor** (profaned): the same armor, corrupted (broken halo, drooping wings). Crafted in the **Corrupted** alignment from a netherite piece and Taint Fragments (helm 4, chestplate 6 + a Taint Heart, leggings 5, boots 3). Same protection as the Seraph; with Iron's Spells each piece gives +6% **Blood** spell power and +25 max mana. All 4 pieces give the **Pact of the Fallen**: **Bloodthirst** (8% of damage dealt returned as health), **Fallen Rage** (below 40% health: +15% damage and blood power) but **−10% healing received** from other sources. Mixing Seraph and Fallen Seraph pieces gives neither set. Upgradable at the Shrine's Forge (taint fragments only).

## Settings

- **config/wavesurvivor/crafting\_fragments.json**: effects, durations, radii, values and the Shrine's craft list (only put the fields to change, then `/ws reload`). Crafting table recipes are ordinary (datapack) recipes.

## Rift Armor

- An **evolving** armor: at the crafting table, an **iron** piece + **8 Rift Dust** gives the **Tier I (Cracked)** piece, as durable as netherite.
- **Tiers II and III** at the **Forge** (Rebirth screen, Armor section): Rift Dust, diamonds, then netherite (and a Rift Shard for the chestplate). Enchantments and durability are kept.
- At Tier III, a **final** choice here, in **Crafting**: **Blessed** (grace fragments and Heart: more armor and health) or **Corrupted** (taint fragments and Heart: more damage and attack speed, a little less health). Three levels per branch.
- **Set** (4 pieces, lowest tier): **Rift Anchor** (capped Rift Effect, less damage from Rift enemies, then the **Rift Wave** below 35% health); **Grace** (immune to the Rift Effect, more healing, the Wave heals allies); **Hunger** (the Wave hurts and withers, life steal).
