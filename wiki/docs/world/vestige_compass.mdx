# Vestige Compass

The **Vestige Compass** guides you to mod structures you have not explored yet, without ever saying what they are.

## Using it

- The needle (golden tip) points to the **nearest unclaimed vestige**: a horde arena, a Seraph Sanctuary or a ruin. With no target it spins randomly.
- **Right-click**: "Unknown vestige N blocks away" (never the name or type).
- **Sneak + right-click**: filter All → Hordes → Sanctuaries → Ruins.

## Claiming a vestige

- Arena: finish its horde. Sanctuary: touch the statue. Ruin: open its chest (Reliquary: open the seal).
- The compass then points to the next one. Tracking is per player (can be shared server-wide).
- It only knows structures that are **already generated**: explore to discover more.

## Crafting

- 1 compass, 4 gold ingots, 2 Grace Shards and 2 Taint Shards (crafting table). Settings in **rules.json**: **vestigeRadius**, **vestigeShared**, **vestigeFilter**.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
