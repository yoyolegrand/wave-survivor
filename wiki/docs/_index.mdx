# Wave Survivor

**A wave-defense mod for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1.** Seal a Runic Altar to a horde, defend the Monolith against waves of monsters, defeat bosses with real mechanics, and walk away with relics, keys and loot.

## What's inside

- **Hordes**: ready-to-play themed hordes (Vanilla and Modded variants), bosses with phases and skills, chaos events, Breaches and Anomalies.
- **Game modes**: Classic, **Kingdom** (build, survive Assaults, recover during Calms), **Boss Rush**, **Endless**, **Daily Challenge**, **Village invasions** and **The Rift**, a full expedition in its own dimension.
- **Progression**: Blessings after each wave, **Renaissance** (prestige ranks, Heritage tree, Rebirth Points), **Relics** in 6 families with set bonuses and 12 legendaries, Vocations for Kingdom roles, achievements and statistics.
- **World**: ready-made arenas, ruins to explore, the Seraph Shrine and its sacred & profane crafting.
- **Fully customizable**: the in-game editor (`/ws editor`) lets you create hordes, monsters, boss skills, blessings and roulette chests without touching a file.
- **English & French**, switchable in game.

## Where to start

New to the mod? Read **Getting started** in the sidebar, then open the in-game guide with `/ws codex`.
