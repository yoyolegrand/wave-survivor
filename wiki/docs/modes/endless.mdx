# Endless mode

A horde played **without limit**: after its last wave it keeps going, ever stronger, until defeat.

## How to play

- At the altar, turn on the **"Endless"** button (unlocked by completing the horde once).
- Normal waves play as usual. After that: **more monsters, more HP, more damage** every wave.
- A **boss** from the horde returns regularly, stronger each time.
- Lives are **shared**: every death costs one. At zero (or if the Monolith falls), it's over.
- PR: normal waves earn their PR as usual, with the victory bonus once the last normal wave is cleared. After that, **+1 PR every 2 waves**.

## Score

- Your score is the number of **waves cleared**. Top scores are ranked per horde: `/ws endless`.
- No save or resume in Endless mode.
- Classic hordes only (Kingdom already has its Assaults). Everything is configurable in the editor (Boss tab › Endless).

## Tiers

- Every **10 waves** (adjustable) from the last normal wave: a **tier chest** (more generous each time), then a 20 s choice during the break.
- **Continue**: you keep **100%** of the winnings at the end, no penalty. **Cash out and stop**: the game ends with **90%** of the winnings (RP).
- In a team: the decision is by **majority** (players who do not vote count as "Continue"), or by the host alone depending on the horde. The leaderboard keeps the wave reached either way.
