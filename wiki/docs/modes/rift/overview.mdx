# The Rift

The final mode: a portal opens onto a dimension of eternal night where four acts follow one another (about 1.5 hours). Requires the **Pehkui** mod.

## Access

- You must have won a classic horde and a Kingdom (and an invasion if the horde requires it). Altar recipe: crying obsidian + amethyst shards.
- The portal forms near the altar: walk through it. Every monster killed in the Rift drops **emeralds** (the kingdom's currency).

## 1. The Horde

- Waves on a small floating island. On death: spectator for a few seconds, then back on the island (your inventory stays intact).

## 2. The Siege

- The island grows and an obsidian fortress rises. The Siege runs on the Kingdom engine: Assaults and Calms, Monolith shop, roles, towers, ramparts, traps.
- **Siege engines** (shop): the catapult tower aims and fires on its own; the celestial ballista is piloted (right-click). Shift + right-click: sheet (upgrade, variant, HP, repair). Monsters and the Titan damage them.

## 3. The Counter-attack

- Enter the fortress: 6 catalysts (light beams) held by chains. Each broken chain spawns guardians. Destroy them all, then the core.
- You can place and break blocks to make your way (but not the fortress walls).

## 4. The Titan

- A colossus rises from the abyss. It takes little damage, except when it is **exposed** (after a stomp, a roar, or when knocked down after a charge).
- Its attacks: meteors (classic, tracking, lava rain, giant), a stomp to jump over, a boulder to shoot down mid-air (engines, arrows), a gaze to dodge behind a wall, a roar, a fist on the Monolith, charges that break the island.
- Weak points (crystals) on its body: each one destroyed takes away some of its HP. Waves pour out of a portal on the bridge.

## End

- Victory or defeat: an exit portal opens near the Monolith.
- Rewards: the RP of a horde and a Kingdom, plus a bonus if the Titan falls, a Sealed Reliquary for every participant, and a podium (top 3 by kills) that gets more.
- Server shutdown: the game is saved at the start of the current phase and can be resumed.

## The Titan's Chest

- When the Titan dies, the Rift casts a beam and a giant chest descends near the centre of the island. Every player present receives 1 **Titan's Key** (an unused key stays valid for a later chest). The chest implodes once every key has been used, or when the Rift closes.
- Loot: diamonds, netherite, grace / taint fragments and hearts, a Sealed Key, **Rift Dust** (48 → 1 Rift Shard at the Forge), a Sealed Reliquary, a Rift Shard, and a 2% chance of a **Rift** family relic (Rift Die, Titan's Seal, Titan Trophy, Titan's Heart). Your Luck counts.
- **Rift Gauge**: each roll without a Rift relic adds +1% Legendary chance to the next ones; it resets to 0 when you get one.
- The family's legendaries (Rift Destiny, Titan's Crown) can only be obtained by fusion at the Forge.

## Rift Armor

- Crafted with **Rift Dust** (crafting table), then upgraded at the Forge (Tiers II and III) and at the Shrine (Blessed or Corrupted branch). As a full set, it caps the **Rift Effect** and reduces damage from Rift enemies. Details: "Sacred & Profane Crafting" page.
