# Rift Islets

During the Siege, islets drift around the island (once it has finished growing, never on the fortress side or the Titan side). While an islet is there, Calms last **60 s** longer solo and **30 s** longer for two players.

## Bridges

- A bridge is bought at the Monolith (Defenses › Engines) with treasury **wood** and **stone**. It builds itself in about 10 s.
- It has **5 sections** with HP: a section at 0 HP collapses into the void. Repair: right-click with **16 stone** in hand near the gap, or use a **Bridge Kit**.
- Stuck on an islet? You can place blocks between the islet and the island (and break the ones you placed).

## Mining Islet

- A decorative mine at the back and **8 veins** that never expire: iron, emerald, an **amethyst** geode and **Essence** crystals (minimum pickaxe like deposits). Loot goes to the treasury.
- Every Siege Calm, half of the depleted veins grow back (75% with a **Miner**), and guards return.
- The main island keeps only 40% of its deposits, with no emerald or amethyst.

## Altar Islet

- Stand in the violet circle to capture the altar (about 30 s solo, faster in a group). A monster in the circle freezes the capture.
- Once captured, pick a bonus in the chat. **Rampart**: the Monolith regains 1% of its HP every 10 s. **Arsenal**: siege engines deal +20% damage. **Prosperity**: +25% stone, wood, iron, emeralds and amethyst.
- Every Assault, monsters march on the altar: if they stay alone in the circle, the altar is retaken and the bonus lost. After the Siege, the bonus stays until the end of the Rift.

## Void Merchant

- He arrives on his islet at the **2nd Calm** of the Siege. Right-click: his shop, paid in **amethyst shards** (Mining Islet, Rift monsters).
- Stock refreshes every Calm; 4 of each consumable per player per Calm. Closed during Assaults (shield) and the Titan fight, open during the Counter-offensive.
- His items: **Void Vial** (8 hearts), Resistance and Strength **Elixirs** (2 min), **Recall Stone** (back to the Monolith), **Bridge Kit**, **Engine Kit** (+50% HP), **Watch Lantern** (monsters glow for 30 s), **Blessing Reroll**, plus a golden apple, a Titan Key, Rift Dust and one Rift Shard per run.

## Chest Islet

- Once per run, during a Siege Calm, a ruined island rises from the void, linked by a free crystal bridge.
- Step on it and elite guardians plus a **Treasure Guardian** awaken. Each guardian slain breaks a chain; once they are all broken, the chest opens: **1 free draw** per player (Luck counts).
- It leaves at the start of the next Assault: an unopened chest is lost.

## Bridgebreaker

- During Assaults, these creatures climb from the void under the bridges and gnaw them: a section collapses in about 15 s, then they move on to the next one.
- Their back is a **weak spot** (double damage from behind or above), and a good knockback throws them into the void: **instant death**. The Watch Lantern makes them glow, even under bridges.
