# Rift Creatures

Creatures native to the Rift (turquoise veins, amethyst crystals). They join the Rift waves and are in the editor's entity catalog (**rift\_geodon**, **rift\_ancreur**, **rift\_bolide**). All of them drop **amethyst shards**.
Rift enemies (and every monster inside the Rift) have a **5% chance** per hit to apply the **Rift Effect**: **−10% healing received per level**, stacks up to 5 levels, each new stack adds 5 s (max 30 s).

## Geodon (mobile tank)

- Slow and very tough. **Ball charge**: between 3 and 16 blocks, it curls up and rolls in a straight line at high speed (10 damage and strong knockback on contact), until it hits an obstacle. Every 10 s.
- **Geode**: right after, it closes up for 4 s, crystals raised: **−75% damage** from the front. **Explosions** and hits **from behind** deal full damage: go around it.

## Anchorer (control)

- Very slow, hits hard. **Anchor**: within 12 blocks, it raises its anchor and slams it down in front of it (1 s warning). The nearest player is **chained**: they get **Slowness V** and can't move more than **6 blocks** away from the anchor for **8 s**.
- A **chain** links the player to the anchor: **one hit** on the chain or the anchor breaks it and frees them. Every 15 s.

## Bolide (spectacular arrival)

- It falls from the sky: a **pink circle** on the ground warns ~1.5 s before; the impact deals 8 damage within 3 blocks.
- **Molten** for the first 15 seconds: fast, its hits set you on fire. Then **cooled** (turquoise cracks): slower, hits weaker and **takes 30% more damage**: wait for it to cool down, or keep it at range.
- During the Titan's **meteor shower**, 1 meteor in 5 is a Bolide rising from the crater (adjustable in the skill).

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
