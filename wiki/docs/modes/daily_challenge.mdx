# Daily Challenge

Every day brings a new challenge: a **difficulty** (Normal, Hard or Nightmare) and **3 mutators**, the same for every player and every server (the day changes at midnight UTC).

## How it works

- At the altar, click **📅 Daily Challenge**: it selects the difficulty and the 3 mutators for you.
- Win the horde (classic or Kingdom) with exactly those settings to earn bonus Renaissance Points, **once per day**.
- `/ws daily` shows today's challenge, your reward and your streak.

## Reward

- **10 RP**, plus **2 RP per streak day** (max +10). A streak counts consecutive days.
- Multiplied by the difficulty: Normal ×1, Hard ×1.5, Nightmare ×2.
- In Kingdom mode, ×1.5 on top.
- Stacks with the usual mutator and difficulty bonuses.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
