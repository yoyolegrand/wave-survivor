# Invasions

A village under attack! Pillagers (and other invaders) march on the bell: protect the villagers until the waves are over.

## How it starts

- **Bad Omen**: enter a village with the effect. A roulette picks: Minecraft's classic raid (with extra rewards) or one of the mod's invasions.
- Operators: `/ws invasion start`, inside a village; `/ws invasion skip` skips the current wave (skipping the last one ends the invasion).

## How it plays

- The bell rings, villagers hide, then a short preparation.
- Several waves with a pause between each; their number depends on the world difficulty and the omen level.
- Invaders open and break doors: defend the houses.
- The bar at the top shows the remaining villagers and the wave.

## Outcome

- **Victory**: Hero of the Village (level from the omen), raiders' loot, Renaissance points, achievements.
- **Defeat**: if too many villagers are lost (half by default) or they are all dead.
- The "vanilla raid" mode keeps Minecraft's waves but is customizable: HP and damage, stats and skills per invader type, plus extra monsters, special waves and a chief.

Everything is configurable in the editor: create a horde in "Invasion" mode.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
