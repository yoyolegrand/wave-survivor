# Kingdom mode

**Gates** open in a ring around the Monolith. Goal: destroy them all.

## The cycle

- **Assault**: the horde pours out of the Gates in squads, with **siege units** (Rams, Sappers, Climbers) targeting your buildings.
- **Calm**: rebuild, trade and prepare for the next Assault. Small breaches still release a few monsters.

## Taking down a Gate

A Gate is sealed. Kill its **Guardians**, then destroy the **Catalyst** that appears near it: the Gate is breached and can be struck. Be quick, it can seal again.

## The King's Gate

The last Gate becomes the King's Gate: its final boss appears on the planned Assault, or earlier if you make it falter. Depending on the horde, it waits for you in its arena.

## Death & survival

Solo, dying loses the game. In multiplayer, you return to the Monolith after 10 s; the game is lost if everyone falls. Inventory is kept during the game.

## Every Calm

Blessings, merchants and roulette chests.

## Building rules

- **Foundations are required**: a defense must rest entirely on solid ground and be reachable by monsters (no pillars). One that loses its support collapses a few seconds after a warning.
- Every Calm brings **guaranteed resources** (deposits and trees) around the kingdom.
- The game is **saved and resumed** with the world: when you come back, a chat message offers to resume the kingdom from the start of its Calm.
