# Map, Compass & Scout

## Kingdom map

Monolith shop › **Town Hall › Map**: a view around the Monolith (3 zoom levels, up to 128 blocks) showing the Monolith, defenses, ramparts, traps, enemy Gates, Catalysts, ore deposits, trees, **corruption hearths** and players. It does not show the buried treasure.

## Kingdom Compass

Bought in the shop (64 ◆). On the map, **click a marker** to point the compass at it, **right-click** to clear. It resets by itself when its target is gone (harvested, destroyed or purified).

## Scout

Each Calm, a scout reports on the next Assault: expected monsters per Gate, special assault, boss, and whether the Gate King awakens. The **🔭 Scout** button shows the report again. The omen and the objective can still change the numbers.

## Assault report

At the start of each Calm: monsters slain (players, soldiers, defenses and traps), treasury gains, building damage and destroyed pieces, and the **MVP**.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
