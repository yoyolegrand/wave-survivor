# Monolith & Town Hall

Right-click the Monolith to open its shop:
**• Upgrades**: Monolith health, regeneration and armor.
**• Defenses**: towers, shrine, barracks, Gleaner.
**• Walls & Traps**: ramparts, gates, traps (and the Builder's charge).
**• 🏛 Town Hall**: each level expands the kingdom (claim +4 blocks) and gives the Monolith +100 HP; some levels unlock level-2 defenses and specializations.
**• ♟ Roles**: role, flasks, omens.

You can only build inside your kingdom (the claim around the Monolith).

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
