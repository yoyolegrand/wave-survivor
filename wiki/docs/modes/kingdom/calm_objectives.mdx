# Calm objectives

Each Calm can offer one optional **objective** (how often is set in the horde editor). Succeeding is rewarded, ignoring it can cost you. Its timer and progress show on the HUD.

**• Supply Convoy**: escort a caravan to the Monolith. Its arrival gives resources.
**• Gate Champion**: an elite and its guard come out of a Gate. Take them down for Essence and loot.
**• Buried Treasure**: a Treasure Compass points to a hidden chest. Follow the HUD arrow and dig.
**• Purification**: corruption hearths appear (they show on the Kingdom map). Break them with any weapon before the Assault: none purified = a stronger next Assault, all purified = a weaker one.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
