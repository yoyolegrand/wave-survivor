# Omens

Augur only. Every Calm, 2 omens are offered (3 with a Vocation talent) (different from the previous time): pick one in chat or in the Roles tab. The omen lasts a single Assault, with an upside and a drawback.

## The omens

### Low Tide

- **-25% units in the Assault.**
- **20% of monsters become elites.**

### Rich Spoils

- **+50% coins dropped by monsters.**
- **+20% units in the Assault.**

### Sleeping Gate

- **One random Gate sends no one.**
- **Other Gates: +30% units.**

### Frenzy

- **+40% units in the Assault.**
- **+6 Breach Essence at the end of the Assault.**

### Iron Rain

- **Each slain monster has a 15% chance to add 1 Iron to the treasury.**
- **25% of groups are siege units.**

### Drowsy Guardians

- **Gate guardians don't re-form for this Assault.**
- **+30% units in the Assault.**

### Royal Hunt

- **Each assault boss slain gives +6 Essence instead of +3.**
- **An extra assault boss appears.**

### Fair Winds

- **Speed I for every player during the Assault.**
- **Monsters get Speed I too.**

### Blood Moon

- **All Essence gained during the Assault is doubled.**
- **Monsters get Strength I and breaches open during the Assault.**

### Offering

- **-35% units in the Assault.**
- **Costs 50 ◆ from the treasury when chosen (refunded if you change your mind).**

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
