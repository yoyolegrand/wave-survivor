# Roles

Each player can take a role, and each role belongs to one player only. Pick it at the altar or in the Monolith's Roles tab; it is final until the end of the horde.

## The 9 roles

### Arcanist

- Sees a light beam above every Catalyst (only them) + an arrow to the nearest one.
- 10% base chance for a 2nd Catalyst to appear at another Gate.
- Purification: sees the hearths (2nd arrow) and purifies them 1.5× faster (base).
- **Drawback: Calm breaches get +50% units; no hearth purified = doubled penalty.**

### Harvester (deposits or trees required)

- Light beam above every deposit and tree (only them) + an arrow to the nearest one.
- Slightly more frequent deposits and trees; Haste I nearby; 15% double loot (base).
- **Drawback: deposits and trees vanish 20% faster.**

### Builder

- Defenses, ramparts, gates and traps -10%, +5% HP on every building.
- Demolition charge (1 per wave, Walls & Traps tab): instantly deals everything left of a Gate's breach.
- **Drawback: defense repairs +25%.**

### Commander

- Each barracks of level 2 or more produces +1 soldier.
- Rallying Horn: plants a banner where they aim, soldiers head there for 40 s (sneak + right-click: recall).

### Quartermaster

- +10% coins on own kills, merchants -8% for them.
- Interest: +3% of the treasury at the end of each Assault (max 60).

### Alchemist

- Each monster they slay gives 1 reagent (3 for an elite).
- During the Calm, brews 5 throwable flasks (frost, fire, healing, storm, bastion) and can upgrade them up to level 2 (3 with a talent), permanently for this kingdom.

### Bounty Hunter

- Gets a contract every Calm (elites, siege units or monsters to slay before the next Calm); fulfilled, it pays Coins (-30% base) and Essence.
- **Drawback: 10% of monsters become elites (+60% HP).**

### Augur

- During the Calm, picks 1 omen out of 2 for the next Assault
- (each omen has its upside and its drawback).

### Scavenger

- Slain siege units drop Wood, Iron and Stone only they can pick up; so do closing Calm breaches.
- Every other horde monster has an 8% chance to leave scrap (Iron or Stone).
- **Drawback: more siege units during Assaults.**

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
