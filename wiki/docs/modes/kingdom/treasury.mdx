# Treasury & resources

The kingdom shares a **common treasury** (panel on the right of the screen).

**• Money ◆**: every emerald picked up goes into it. « Withdraw 10 ◆ » (Monolith) turns it back into emeralds for merchants.
**• Stone, Wood, Iron**: +1 per natural block broken (placed blocks don't count). A destroyed deposit gives Stone and Iron, a felled tree gives Wood (based on size).
**• Breach Essence**: Catalyst destroyed +3, Guardians defeated +5, breach exhausted +5, boss +3. Deposits and trees give some too.

These resources pay for upgrades, defenses, walls, traps and the Town Hall.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
