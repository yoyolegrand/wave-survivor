# Alchemy

Alchemist only. Each monster killed gives reagents; during the Calm, spend them (Roles tab) to brew or upgrade throwable flasks (levels 1 to 3). Right-click to throw.

## Flasks

### Frost Flask

- Slowness III and freezing on monsters.
- Lv. 1: radius 4, 6 s
- Lv. 2: radius 5, 8 s
- Lv. 3: radius 6, 10 s + Weakness

### Fire Flask

- Damage and fire on monsters.
- Lv. 1: 4 damage, 6 s of fire, radius 4
- Lv. 2: 6 damage, 8 s, radius 4.5
- Lv. 3: 8 damage, 10 s, radius 5

### Healing Flask

- Heals players and soldiers.
- Lv. 1: +8 HP, Regeneration 5 s, radius 5
- Lv. 2: +12 HP, 7 s, radius 6
- Lv. 3: +16 HP, 10 s, radius 7

### Storm Flask

- A lightning bolt strikes monsters and reveals them (glowing 5 s).
- Lv. 1: 6 damage, radius 3
- Lv. 2: 8 damage, radius 3.5
- Lv. 3: 10 damage, radius 4

### Bastion Flask

- Repairs buildings and shields allies (Resistance I).
- Lv. 1: +15% HP, 6 s, radius 6
- Lv. 2: +25% HP, 8 s, radius 7
- Lv. 3: +35% HP, 10 s, radius 8

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
