# Vocations

Every Kingdom role has its own **Mastery** (levels 0 to 20) and a **talent tree**. The more you play a role, the stronger it gets: base role effects are lower than before, and talents bring them back — and beyond.

## Mastery

- Play a role in a Kingdom game to earn **Mastery XP**: 10 per Assault survived, plus XP for what the role really does (closing Catalysts, building, brewing flasks, completing contracts, closing breaches…).
- The XP is paid out when the game ends: **×2 on victory**, **×0.5 on defeat**. You get a message, and level-ups show in the event feed.
- Each level gives **1 talent point** for that role (20 at most).

## Talent trees

- 3 branches of 4 talents per role; some talents have several ranks. A column's 2nd / 3rd / 4th talent needs **2 / 5 / 8 points** spent in the role's tree (all branches together) and the previous talents of the column taken.
- **Ultimate talent**: spend 15 points, then 150 RP (paid once per role) to pick one of the two ultimates.
- Resetting a role's tree costs **50 RP** and gives all points back. Spent RP count towards Renaissance.
- Talents can only be changed **outside a game**, and only apply in Kingdom games, to the player who holds the role.
- A talent marked Coming soon is not active yet and cannot be bought.

## Where

- **Renaissance Altar → Vocations**: the role grid, then each role's tree (click a talent to add a rank). Hover a talent for its details.
- **Monolith → Roles**: your Mastery in your role, the XP earned this game, and a read-only view of the full tree.
- Server owners: every value (XP curve, ranks, costs, base effects) is in **vocations.json**; operators can test with `/ws vocation`.

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
