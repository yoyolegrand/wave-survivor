# Defenses & traps

Your buildings have HP: monsters, especially siege units, go after them. Upgrade them with money (Town Hall required) and repair them.

## Sheet & barracks orders

Right-click a defense to see its sheet (level, HP, specialization). On a barracks, give its soldiers an order: **Hold**, **Monolith** or **Patrol**. The **Commander** also has **Follow me** (soldiers escort you, +20% damage and speed within 12 blocks) and the **Rallying Horn** (right-click: plants a banner where you aim, soldiers go there for 60 s; sneak + right-click recalls them).

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
