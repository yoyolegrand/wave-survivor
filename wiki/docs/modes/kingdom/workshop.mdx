# Repair Workshop

The **Repair Workshop** (a 2×1 block, bought in the Monolith shop) slowly repairs your gear.

- Drop damaged armor, weapons and tools into it. Items stay **reserved to the player** who dropped them.
- Level 1: 2 slots, level 2: 4 slots, level 3: 6 slots, and it repairs faster at each level.
- Right-click it to open its sheet and choose a specialization when one is available.

## Repair all

In the Monolith shop, **🔧 Repair all** repairs every damaged defense, most damaged first, at the manual repair price. The first click shows a **quote**: click again within 10 s to confirm. It stops when the treasury runs out.
