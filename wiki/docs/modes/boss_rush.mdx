# Boss Rush

A horde played with **only its bosses**, back to back. No monster waves, special waves, chaos events or Breaches.

## How to play

- At the altar, click **⚔ Boss Rush** (click again for the **Gauntlet**), then launch the horde.
- It unlocks once you have finished that horde (editable per horde).
- Available for hordes that have bosses, classic or Kingdom.

## Rules

- The team shares a few **lives**: every death costs one, and with none left the Boss Rush is lost.
- Between two bosses: a short pause (20 s by default) with merchants, supply chests and blessings, and you recover your health.
- A **timer** runs the whole time. Best times are saved: `/ws bossrush` shows them.
- **Gauntlet**: each boss is stronger than the previous one.

## Kingdom mode

- Every Assault is a boss, with no monsters or siege units. Calms stay: build and repair between bosses.
- The Gates stay as scenery and as the places where bosses appear: no Guardians, no Catalysts.
- Victory: defeat the last boss. You do not have to destroy the Gates.

## Rewards

- The usual boss loot, plus Renaissance Points for each boss defeated and a bonus on victory.
- No first-victory bonus, and it does not count as finishing the horde.
- Works with difficulties and mutators.

Operators can tune everything in `/ws editor`, tab **Boss**, button **👑 Boss Rush settings**: lives, pause, which bosses, Gauntlet strength, rewards, time limit…
