# Getting started

**Wave Survivor**: defend your position against waves of monsters.

## Starting a horde

- Place a **Runic Altar** and offer it a horde's ingredients to seal it.
- Right-click the sealed altar: pick the variant, **difficulty** and **mutators**, then launch.
- Shift + click (twice within 5 s) unbinds the altar, except during a horde.
- Some hordes are locked: their conditions are shown at the altar.

## During the horde

- If the horde enables defense, protect and repair the **Monolith**: if it falls, the horde wins and the altar is desecrated (it must be reconsecrated).
- An interrupted game can be resumed at the start of its wave.

## Commands

`/ws codex`: this guide · `/ws info`: information · `/ws editor`: editor (OP)

<Callout type="info">This page mirrors the in-game **Codex** (`/ws codex`). Values are the defaults: most of them can be changed in the config or the in-game editor.</Callout>
