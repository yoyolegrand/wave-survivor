# 1.8.0 — Changelog (new features only, to post later)

## Blessings
- Blessings are now data, not code: categories (Life, Armor, Force...) with Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary levels, plus a full in-game Blessings editor (categories, rarity arrows, per-level availability, any attribute of the game or your mods).
- Rarity matters in the draw: three distinct categories per menu, weights shifting towards rarer levels as waves go on, and Luck raising the odds.
- Rerolls and bans (2 + 1 to start, +1 of each every 5 waves), with a family choice on reroll and a "Keep a card" option.
- 8 special blessings: Execution, Dawn, Magnet, Banner, Blood Link and three Pacts (Shattered Glass, Berserker, Burden).
- Curses (rare red cards), family synergies (3 / 5 / 7), a "My bonuses" screen, stat previews on cards and a settings screen.
- Blessing packs (export / import to share) and per-world statistics.
- Luck also improves Offering Brazier rewards, roulette chests and monster loot.

## Seraph Shrine
- New structure with a faith gauge (Pure to Corrupted), offerings, shards of grace and taint (Shard, Fragment, Heart) and a Forge that gives weapons and armor up to 3 upgrades (12 consecrations, 12 corruptions).
- Upgraded gear gets a colored name, a full tooltip and an animated slot frame.
- A tainted statue calls Rootlings and a Stone Brute.

## Ready-made arenas
- Necropolis of the Fallen Kings and The Infernal Breach.
- Void Islet (floating islands, End horde), Sunken Reef (shallow seas), Temple of Ra (Burning Sands), Frost Bastion (Frozen Peaks, shelters count against storms) and Arcane Circle (requires Iron's Spells).
- Crown Vale: a 181 x 181 Kingdom arena for the Pillagers, with four fortified camps (a Gate at the center of each), a rocky hill, a quarry, a lake, a shallow river with fords, forests built with real trees and ominous banners. It places the Kingdom Gates itself without touching your horde settings.
- Natural generation for every arena (rotation, biomes, terrain blending), plus /ws arena list and /ws arena place.
- Each arena has its own reward chest and spawn points.

## Relics
- Legendary relics can now be upgraded in the Forge by combining two identical legendaries (15 then 30 RP, same as other relics).
  - Level I: the two original relics act at level II (as before).
  - Levels II and III: they act at level III.
  - Unique effects scale (x1 / x1.5 / x2): Banner aura radius, Hunter's Trophy bonus, Chalice healing and absorption, Gates key damage, Watcher's invulnerability duration and reveal radius, Merchant King refund chance, Skeleton Key drop chance, Seasons burn and freeze duration.
- Tooltips now show the real numbers for the relic's level (e.g. +25 / +37.5 / +50 %) instead of "x1.5".
- Hold Shift on a legendary to see the real effects of its two original relics.

## Event feed
- Horde, Kingdom, Boss Rush, Endless and Invasion announcements (wave start, wave cleared, bonuses, boss arrival, Monolith at 50 % / 25 %, blessing chosen...) now appear in the left-side event feed instead of the chat. Major events use a larger "important" card.

## New creatures
- Rootling, Stone Brute and Voidmantis (HD models) with spawn eggs.

## Performance and tools
- /ws perf to measure wave performance.
- Lighter particles on idle roulette chests and altars (only near a player) and faster Curios relic checks.
