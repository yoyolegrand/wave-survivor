# Compatibility

| | |
|---|---|
| **Minecraft** | 1.20.1 |
| **Forge** | 47.x or newer |
| **Required mods** | none: the Vanilla hordes work on their own |

## Optional mods (detected automatically)

| Mod | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| **Curios API** | the Relic slot for relics (without Curios, relics are equipped at the Renaissance Altar) |
| **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks** | the *Arcane Conclave* horde, the "Iron's Spell" skill for custom monsters, Rift Armor bonuses |
| **Epic Fight** | compatible |
| **Pehkui** | **required for The Rift** (the Titan is a scaled-up entity) |
| **Apothic Attributes** | its attributes can be used in blessings |
| Other content mods (L_Ender's Cataclysm, Alex's Caves, Travel Optics, Variants & Ventures…) | the **Modded** variants of the hordes |

If a horde variant needs a mod you don't have, its **Start** button is greyed out and the missing mods are listed at the altar.

## Any mod's monsters and items

The in-game editor accepts **any entity and any item** from any mod: monsters, bosses, loot, blessing attributes and roulette chest rewards are not limited to vanilla.
