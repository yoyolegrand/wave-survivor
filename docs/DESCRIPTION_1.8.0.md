# 🛡️ Wave Survivor

**Bind a Runic Altar to a horde, defend the Monolith, survive the waves — or build a whole kingdom around it.**

Wave Survivor turns Minecraft into a wave-defense game you can play solo or with friends. Hordes grow stronger every wave, bosses arrive, chaos events shake the arena, and between fights you trade, open mystery chests and choose blessings. Everything — hordes, waves, bosses, blessings, loot, merchants — can be edited in game, no file editing required.

---

## 🆕 New in 1.8.0 — Blessings Reborn, Seraph Shrine & Arenas

- 🙏 **Blessings, rebuilt from the ground up**: rarity levels (Common → Legendary) that really matter in the draw, **Luck**, **rerolls and bans**, **8 special blessings**, **curses**, **family synergies**, previews, packs you can share, and a full in-game **Blessings editor**.
- 🕊 **Seraph Shrine**: a new structure with a faith gauge from Pure to Corrupted. Make offerings, collect **shards of grace and taint**, and upgrade your weapons and armor at its **Forge** (12 consecrations, 12 corruptions).
- 🏟 **Eight ready-made arenas** that generate naturally in the world: Necropolis of the Fallen Kings, The Infernal Breach, Void Islet, Sunken Reef, Temple of Ra, Frost Bastion, Arcane Circle — and **Crown Vale**, a giant **181 × 181 Kingdom battlefield**.
- 💍 **Legendary relics can now be upgraded** (levels I–III) by combining two identical ones, and tooltips show the real numbers.
- 📢 **Event feed**: wave starts, bosses and Kingdom events now slide in on the left of the screen instead of spamming the chat.
- 🪲 **New creatures**: Rootling, Stone Brute and Voidmantis, with high-definition models.

## ⚔️ Ways to play

### Classic Hordes
- Waves of monsters that scale with players and difficulty
- Bosses with real mechanics, special waves, breaches to close, merchants between waves
- Mystery chests (roulette), blessings, and a full horde progression to unlock
- Chaos events: ore deposits, fantasy trees, storms, sandstorms and earthquakes
- Hordes include the Necropolis, the Nether, the End, the Depths, **Frozen Peaks** and **Burning Sands**

### ♾️ Endless Mode, 🏴 Boss Rush & 📅 Daily Challenge
- **Endless**: keep going after the last wave until the team falls — a boss returns every few waves
- **Boss Rush**: face the bosses back to back, in hordes and in Kingdom
- **Daily Challenge**: a new difficulty and three mutators every day, with bonus Renaissance Points
- **Village invasions**: walk into a village with Bad Omen and defend it from custom raids
- 24 achievements and a Statistics screen

### 🏰 Kingdom Mode
- Your Monolith sits at the heart of a **claim**; enemy **Gates** open in a ring around it
- Alternate between **Calm** (gather, build, complete objectives) and **Assaults**
- Archer and Mage towers, Barracks with orders, Repair Workshops, Offering Brazier, Gleaner Tower, walls, gates and **traps**
- Siege units (Rams, Sappers, Climbers, Standard-bearers), a shared **treasury**, **player roles**, a Town Hall to upgrade
- Kingdom map, Scout, shared lives and **Calm objectives** (Convoy, Champion, Buried Treasure, Purification)

## 🔮 Relics & Renaissance
- **Relics in 6 families** with set bonuses, levels I–III, Forge upgrades and fusions into **legendary relics** — Curios slots supported
- **Renaissance** (prestige): a Heritage tree, ranks I–V, Renaissance Points earned by playing, and a shop

---

## 🛠️ Fully editable in game
- Visual **horde editor**: waves, entities, bosses, skills, chaos events, loot, merchants, Kingdom settings
- **Blessings editor**, starter templates, validation, wave preview, import/export (`.zip`)
- Collaborative editing and an in-game **Codex** that explains every mechanic
- Altar layout screen: place chests and merchants on a top-down grid
- Your changes are never overwritten when the mod updates
